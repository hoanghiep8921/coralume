Science Meets Stewardship

Nestled in the heart of Vietnam's most celebrated marine reserve, the **Nha Trang Coral Stewardship Center** serves as the operational pulse of Coralume. In exclusive partnership with local oceanographers and the Marine Institute, we've established a sanctuary where technology and nature converge.

Our mission is simple yet profound: to reverse decades of degradation through accelerated coral growth techniques. By merging AI-driven monitoring with community-led adoption, we ensure every fragment planted has a documented journey from nursery to thriving reef.

Together with the people of Nha Trang, we aren't just planting coral; we are rebuilding local livelihoods and securing a blue heritage for generations to come.
